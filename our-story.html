2019 — 2026

How it started

Established in 2019, Vrijheid Leather Works began in Windhoek as a simple hobby, born from a passion for working with leather and creating something with our own hands.

What started small steadily grew through dedication, craftsmanship and the support of our customers, becoming a fully fledged business in April 2026.

Vrijheid means freedom

Why Vrijheid

Our mission is to make products that become part of the lives of the people who use them; products that complement their individuality, their work, their ambitions and the freedom to pursue whatever path they choose.

Whether it is something personal, something for the workplace, or something made specifically for a business, every piece is created with purpose, character and longevity in mind.

Windhoek CBD

Made in Namibia

Today, Vrijheid Leather Works is proudly based in the CBD of Windhoek, where we design and handcraft quality leather products using carefully selected Namibian materials, including genuine Namibian leather, with a purpose that goes beyond simply making a product.

Our products are made in Namibia, with Namibian materials, for Namibians — while carrying a quality and character that can proudly travel beyond our borders.

Johan & Ina

A family craft

Vrijheid is a family-built business, created through the combined skills and dedication of Johan and his wife, Ina.

Together, our different skills and perspectives allow us to serve both sides of the market — from individual customers looking for something personal and distinctive, to corporate clients seeking quality, practical and proudly Namibian products.

What we believe

Built for people

We believe in handmade craftsmanship, honest materials and products built to last.

Most importantly, we believe in creating for our own people.

At Vrijheid, every piece is made with the same simple belief:

Your freedom is yours to pursue.
Our craft is to help you carry it.
