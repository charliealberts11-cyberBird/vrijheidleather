    Our Purpose

    Made by hand.
    Made with purpose.

    Vrijheid Leather Works began in Windhoek in 2019 as a hobby — a love of working with leather and making something real with our own hands. Through dedication, craftsmanship and the support of our customers, it grew into a fully fledged business in April 2026.

    Today, Johan and Ina design and handcraft every piece in the Windhoek CBD, using carefully selected Namibian materials — including genuine Namibian leather — for personal and corporate customers alike.
