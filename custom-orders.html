The process

How it works

Every product is handcrafted and, to ensure quality, the minimum waiting period per product is 2 weeks.

  1. 01

    Share your idea

    Tell us what you have in mind. A sketch, a photo, a logo or a few words is enough to start.

  2. 02

    Discuss the details

    We talk through size, leather, colour, engraving, quantity and timing with you.

  3. 03

    Approve the direction

    Once you are happy with the plan and the price, we confirm your order.

  4. 04

    We craft it by hand

    Your piece is made by hand in Windhoek. Please allow a minimum of 2 weeks per product.
